# Masinloc search-intent ownership

Last researched: 2026-08-25

This is an editorial routing document, not a keyword quota. Each search intent
has one primary public page. Supporting pages may answer a narrower question
and link to the owner; they must not be retitled into near-duplicates.

## What the live results show

Broad Masinloc results are split among Wikipedia, PhilAtlas, map/social
profiles and government references. Travel results are dominated by Trip.com,
Tripadvisor, Expedia/Agoda, Facebook pages and older personal blogs. Several
aggregator lists mix Masinloc with places in other Zambales towns or even
Manila. Business discovery is fragmented across social profiles, Tripadvisor
and generic local directories. History and church results mix Wikipedia,
tourism pages and unsourced blogs. Language results are stronger academically:
UP Linguistics, SIL and Glottolog are important external authorities.

That creates an opportunity for a source-led local publisher, but not a reason
to manufacture pages. The site should win by being more specific, better
sourced and clearer about uncertainty.

## Cannibalization rules

1. Homepage owns the broad place name. No second “complete guide to Masinloc.”
2. Destinations owns tourist-spots/list intent. The eight-place Discover story
   owns interpretation by geography, not another list SERP title.
3. Sambal Tina owns the language/dictionary entity. Discover and Bulletin
   answer preservation and definition questions, respectively.
4. Verified History owns broad history. Bulletin carries research and
   verification; Discover translates that evidence for a general reader.
5. Marketplace owns business discovery. Detail pages own business names and
   truthful supplied categories; category pages wait for sufficient inventory.
6. New URLs require distinct intent, reliable evidence and a materially better
   answer than an existing page. Keyword variants alone never justify a page.
