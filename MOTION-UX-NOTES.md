# Modern UX and Motion Rules

This pass keeps the approved Masinloc content architecture and image assets intact while adding progressive interaction polish.

- Motion must clarify hierarchy or navigation, not decorate every element.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must disable reveal, parallax, and transition effects.
- The approved `assets/stage1/masinloc-hero.avif` remains untouched.
- Public navigation becomes persistent after scrolling while inner editorial pages use a sticky white navigation surface.
- Scroll reveals are progressive enhancement only; no-JS content must remain visible.
- Masinloc Connect visual overrides must not change submission logic, saved drafts, validation, or publishing safeguards.
- Do not expose unfinished Stage 2+ routes through this polish layer.
