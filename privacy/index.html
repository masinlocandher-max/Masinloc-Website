Masinloc Connect

Privacy

Masinloc Connect is designed so people can browse community information and send an incident report without creating an account. Registration is optional and is used only when you choose personalized features such as saved jobs, saved content, profile information or a Signature Resume.

Effective: September 1, 2026Applies to the website and mobile app

Information you can use without an account

You can browse public Masinloc information, Marketplace listings, Mabayani, Sambal Tina, Discover content and public job opportunities without registering. Public content displayed in the mobile app is sourced from the same Masinloc Connect website and platform.

Optional account information

If you choose Continue with Email, the platform processes your email address for secure sign-in. You may optionally add a display name and location or barangay. If you use career features, you may provide information such as your name, target role, skills, career summary, availability and resume details.

Saved jobs, saved Marketplace entries, saved Mabayani chapters and saved Sambal Tina words are stored as references to the original content rather than duplicate copies of that content.

Emergency and assistance reports

An account is not required to send an incident report. A report may include the incident type, your description, the selected responder destination, optional contact details, optional barangay or landmark, and location coordinates if you explicitly allow location access or choose to attach a GPS fix.

If a report is created while the device is offline, it can be stored locally on that device until connectivity is available. The interface distinguishes a report saved only on the device from a report actually received by the emergency system.

Emergency reports, responder messages, status history and related operational records may be retained when needed for safety, incident handling, accountability, dispute resolution, security or lawful recordkeeping. Deleting a Masinloc Connect account does not automatically erase operational emergency records.

Location

The app does not require continuous background location tracking. Device location is requested for the incident-reporting flow when location can help responders identify where assistance is needed. If you do not provide a GPS location, you can enter a barangay or nearby landmark instead.

The weather summary shown on the app Home screen uses the Masinloc town-center location for a public weather request; it does not require your personal device location.

Jobs and external services

Masinloc Connect can display public job opportunities supplied or verified through trusted job providers. When you choose to apply, the app may open the provider's external application page. Information you submit on that external site is governed by that provider's own privacy practices.

Service providers and security

Masinloc Connect uses Supabase for authentication, database services and server functions. Public weather information is obtained from Open-Meteo. The application also loads public editorial data and approved media from the Masinloc Connect website. Access controls and database policies are used to separate public content from user-owned account information.

Masinloc Connect does not use the mobile app to sell personal information or to run third-party behavioral advertising.

Account deletion

You can initiate account deletion inside the mobile app from Profile → Delete account, or use the public deletion page below. Account deletion removes the active login identity and optional mobile profile, saved-content, saved-job and career/resume data. Emergency and required transaction or audit records may be retained where necessary without an active personal account.

Questions and corrections

For privacy questions, corrections or concerns, use the contact and Help Desk channels published on the official Masinloc Connect website. Do not include passwords, one-time sign-in links or other authentication secrets in a message.
