Contact

Send us a message.

A person reads every message that comes through here and replies to you directly. There is no mailing list and no automated response.

Contact form

Your message and contact details go to the Masinloc team only. They are not published, not shared, and not added to any list.

What happens next

  1. 1It reaches the team. Your message lands in the private review console. Nothing about it is public.
  2. 2A person reads it. Not a bot, and not an autoresponder.
  3. 3We reply to you. By email, or by phone if you left a number and prefer that.

Better handled elsewhere

  • Listing a business, sending a story, or adding a professional profile: use Masinloc Connect, which is built for it.
  • A Sambal Tina word or a correction to an entry: send it from the dictionary so it reaches the right review queue.
  • Who runs this platform and how information is handled: read the trust and privacy page.

This is an independent community platform. For official announcements, permits, public records and government services, contact the Municipal Government of Masinloc through its own official channels.
