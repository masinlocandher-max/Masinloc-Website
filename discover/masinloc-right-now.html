Masinloc, Right Now

The current numbers and the people currently holding office, with the date each one was last checked and the source it came from. This page is meant to be corrected.

Published 2026-08-23

Every other article on this site tries to be true for a long time. This one cannot be. Populations are recounted, officials are elected, and a page that quietly keeps displaying last term's mayor is worse than a page that never existed, because it looks maintained.

So the time-sensitive facts live in one place, each with the source it came from and the date somebody last checked it against that source. When a figure below is out of date, it is a small correction to one file rather than a hunt through a dozen paragraphs.

"Last verified" means a person opened the source and confirmed the value. It does not mean the value changed on that date, and it does not move because the website was redeployed. If a figure below is stale, the honest fix is to check it and change the date, not to change the date.

The population figure comes from the 2024 Census of Population, whose counts were declared official by presidential proclamation. Census figures are revised rarely and deliberately; the year attached to the number matters more than the number's age.

The officials are those elected in the national and local elections of 12 May 2025, for terms running to 2028. Masinloc has been continuously led by women since 2010, a fact this project states plainly and without embellishment, because the record supports the sentence and does not support the larger claims that are sometimes built on top of it.
