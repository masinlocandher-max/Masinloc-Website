Masinloc in Numbers, Right Now

A dated record of Masinloc's population, barangays and elected leaders, with a source and verification date for every value that can change.

Published 2026-08-23 · Updated 2026-08-25

56,579 is a precise number with an expiry label: it is Masinloc's population in the 2024 census, not a permanent description of the town. Names of elected officials age faster. This page treats changing facts as dated records instead of evergreen prose.

Each value below carries its source and the day it was checked. Other articles link here instead of copying the figures, so one verified correction updates the site's shared understanding rather than leaving contradictory mayors or census totals across several URLs.

"Last verified" means a person opened the source and confirmed the value. It does not mean the value changed on that date, and it does not move because the website was redeployed. If a figure below is stale, the honest fix is to check it and change the date, not to change the date.

The Philippine Statistics Authority's municipal record supplies both the 56,579 total and the thirteen-barangay count. Its barangay table also makes the scale visible: Inhobol has 10,225 residents, while North Poblacion has 1,664. Those figures belong to the same 2024 census and should always be read with that year attached.

The mayor and vice mayor shown here are those reported elected in the 12 May 2025 local elections for the 2025–2028 term. This is a civic reference, not an official municipal service: appointments, vacancies or later changes require a new check rather than an assumption that an election-night list remains current forever.
