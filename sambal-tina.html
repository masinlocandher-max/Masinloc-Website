How we made this

We copied the archive faithfully

The archival record is preserved as its own layer. Where a historical reading is shaky we tell you, and we keep the page reference so the source can be checked directly.

When we are not sure, we say so

Some of the old text is damaged. Rather than guess at a spelling that looks about right, we flag it and leave it open. A neat invention would stick around forever in a language that has very little written down.

We keep living usage separate

Masinloqueños still use forms that may differ from the archival transcription. When a form is explicitly confirmed in current project work, we publish it as User-confirmed instead of silently rewriting the archive. Forms such as ayama and cabatwan can therefore sit beside archival variants with both histories intact.

    Who made this

    This dictionary is the work of the Mabayani Project by FMB.

    The archival layer was built by reading the source across three separate layers, cross-checking the main body against the printed index, reviewing doubtful letters, and assigning an honest source status to every entry.

    The living layer records forms explicitly confirmed in current project work. It is deliberately kept separate so contemporary Masinloc usage can grow without erasing or falsely rewriting the historical record.

    The fine print

    Compilation, transcription, review and presentation © 2026 Mabayani Project by FMB.

    Archival entries cite the archive page from which they were transcribed. User-confirmed living entries are labeled separately and do not receive an archive page unless the same exact form is independently supported by the archive.

    What the labels mean

    User-confirmed
    A form explicitly confirmed in current Masinloc project work. It may also be archive-backed, or it may be living usage published separately from the archival transcription.
    Well supported
    We found the archival form in more than one source layer, or matched it against the printed index. 3,241 archive entries.
    Readable
    Clear enough to read in the archival main body, but not double-checked elsewhere yet. 1,884 archive entries.
    Needs another look
    A letter in the old text is damaged or unclear, so treat the archival spelling as unsettled. 97 entries.
