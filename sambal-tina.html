How we made this

We copied it faithfully

The archive is the real thing and we have not tried to improve on it. Where our reading is shaky we tell you, and we give you the page so you can go and look for yourself.

When we are not sure, we say so

Some of the old text is damaged. Rather than guess at a spelling that looks about right, we flag it and leave it open. A neat invention would stick around forever in a language that has very little written down.

There is still work to do

97 entries are still unsettled, and we have left them out in the open. If you speak Sambal Tina or can reach the archive, you can help us close them — and we would be glad of the help.

    Who made this

    This dictionary is the work of the Mabayani Project by FMB.

    Everything here was done by hand: reading the archive across three separate layers, cross-checking the main body against the printed index, squinting at every doubtful letter, and giving each entry an honest rating. Those ratings, and the judgement calls behind them, are ours.

    It keeps growing. Entries move up as readings get settled, and everyone who helps gets named.
