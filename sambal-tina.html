Scope and method

Transcribed, not reconstructed

The archive is the record. This edition transcribes it and states where the transcription is uncertain, giving the page so that any reading can be checked against the original.

Uncertain glyphs are left uncertain

Parts of the record survive as damaged text. Those entries are published under review rather than resolved by guesswork. A plausible invention would enter the record permanently for a language with few written sources.

The record is incomplete

97 entries remain unresolved and are marked as such. They are listed openly so that readers with the language, or with access to the archive, can settle them.

    Compilation

    The Masinloc Sambal Tina Dictionary is a record of the Mabayani Project by FMB.

    The work published here is the project’s own: reading the source archive across three separate layers, cross-linking the main body against the printed index, reviewing every ambiguous glyph, and assigning each entry a source status and a confidence rating. Those ratings, and the review decisions behind them, belong to the project.

    The edition is open. Entries are reclassified as readings are settled, and contributors are recorded.
