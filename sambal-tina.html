How to use the reading guide. This is our Pronunciation / source orthography guide, not a generated phonetic transcript. The word itself remains in the resolved source spelling. In the modern Tina Sambal orthography described by the 1988 dictionary, a word-medial hyphen marks a glottal stop, so the card's reading line shows that point as ʔ, a brief catch in the voice. The orthography uses three vowels, A, I and O. Stress and word-final glottal stops are distinguished with accent marks in the original system. When those marks are not preserved in the resolved transcription, we do not invent IPA or stress.
