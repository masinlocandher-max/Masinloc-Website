The Masinloc Sambal Tina Dictionary

Compiled, transcribed and verified by the Sambal Tina Documentation Project

Sambal Tina is the language of Masinloc. This is our working dictionary of 5,222 entries: read from the printed record, cross-checked layer against layer, and rated for how far each reading can be trusted. Every entry tells you which page it came from and how confident we are that we have read it correctly.

What this is, and what it is not

Our reading, checked against the record

We transcribe from the printed record rather than replacing it. Where our reading of a word is uncertain, we say so and give you the page number so you can check it yourself.

We do not guess at spelling

Some entries survive only as damaged text. We publish them flagged rather than quietly inventing a plausible spelling. A wrong guess would enter the record for a language that has few written sources.

It is not finished

97 entries still need someone to look at the printed page. If you read Sambal Tina and can help settle them, that work is genuinely valuable.

A few words to start with

Everyday vocabulary, limited to entries the source supports well. Useful whether you are visiting Masinloc or coming home to it.

    Who made this

    The Masinloc Sambal Tina Dictionary is compiled, transcribed and verified by the Sambal Tina Documentation Project.

    The work published here is ours: reading the record across three separate layers, cross-linking the main dictionary body against the printed Tina index, reviewing every ambiguous glyph, and assigning each entry a source status and a confidence rating. Those ratings, the QA decisions behind them, and this presentation are the project’s own.

    It is ongoing. Entries move as readings are settled, and contributors are credited.

    Cited source

    English–Tina Sambal–Pilipino Dictionary (1988). The printed dictionary remains the authority for any disputed reading, and is cited by page on every entry so that anyone can check our work against it.

    How to read an entry

    Well supported
    Confirmed across more than one layer of the source, or cross-checked against the printed Tina index. 3,241 entries.
    Readable
    Supported by the main dictionary body and readable, but not independently cross-checked. 1,884 entries.
    Needs a source check
    A glyph in the original is damaged or ambiguous. Treat the spelling as unsettled and check the page. 97 entries.
