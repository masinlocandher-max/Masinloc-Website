How we build it

We gather it

We collect Sambal Tina words, meanings, phrases, and everyday usage so they can be easier to find, learn, and pass on.

We verify it

We review entries, compare details, and check unfamiliar or conflicting forms before treating them as reliable. When something still needs work, we keep reviewing it.

We improve it together

You can contribute a word, meaning, example, or correction. You do not need to check first if it already exists. Send what you know and our admin review will sort, compare, and verify it.

    Made for Masinloc

    The Sambal Tina Dictionary is built and maintained by the Mabayani Project by FMB.

    It is a living digital collection. We gather, organize, review, verify, and improve entries so Sambal Tina can be easier to learn, use, and share.

    Community knowledge matters here. Helpful submissions and corrections are reviewed by the team, and approved contributors can be credited by name with their permission.

    Join the work

    Know a word we should add? Found a spelling or meaning that needs attention? Send it. You do not have to solve everything before contributing.

    What the labels mean

    Community-confirmed
    A form that has also been explicitly confirmed in current Masinloc project work.
    Verified
    An entry with stronger verification and cross-checking.
    Reviewed
    An entry that is readable and usable, with further checking still welcome.
    Review in progress
    An entry that still needs closer checking before we treat every detail as settled.
