Be Mesmerized

History on this platform should be readable without becoming careless. Entries can distinguish documented facts, oral memory, interpretation, and unresolved questions.

See the source standard →Contribute a correction →
SOURCE STANDARD / 01

Clear about what we know.

  • DocumentedBacked by a source that can be checked.
  • Oral memoryAttributed as community memory, not presented as settled fact.
  • InterpretationSeparated from the underlying evidence.
  • UnresolvedMarked clearly when the record is incomplete or disputed.
