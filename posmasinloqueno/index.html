Masinloc POS

Restaurant tools for Masinloc, built here.

Masinloc POS is a mobile-first ordering, point-of-sale and kitchen system for restaurants, cafés and food businesses in Masinloc, Zambales. It is still being built. This page is a placeholder, not a working app.

What it will do

  • Guest ordering. Scan a table QR and order under your name. No account needed.
  • Payments. Cash, GCash, Maya, QR Ph or card at the counter, verified by staff before the kitchen sees the order.
  • Order queue and POS. Walk-in sales and online orders in one list.
  • Kitchen display. Paid, preparing, ready, completed.
  • Tracking and order chat. Customers follow their own order and message the store about it.
  • Guest loyalty. Points earned without signing up for anything.
  • Marketplace listing. A verified business also appears in Masinloc Marketplace.

Where it stands

Built. The ordering, payment-verification, kitchen and loyalty data model, and the link that gives a verified business its Marketplace listing.

Not built. The merchant and customer apps. Nothing on this page is connected to a live store yet.

Registration is not open

No business can sign up here yet, and this page collects nothing. When onboarding opens it will be announced through Masinloc Connect. Until then, please do not send business or personal details to this address.
