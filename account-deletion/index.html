Masinloc Connect Privacy

Account control

Delete your account.

Use the email connected to Masinloc Connect. We will send a secure sign-in link before any deletion can happen. Browsing and incident reporting never require an account.

Verify your account

Delete Masinloc Connect account

This removes the active account and optional profile, saved items, saved jobs and career/resume data. Emergency reports and required transaction or audit records may be retained where needed for safety or recordkeeping without an active personal account.

This action cannot be undone. If you return later, you may create a new Masinloc Connect account with an email address.

You can also delete inside the app

Open Masinloc Connect, go to your Profile, and choose Delete account. Both paths use the same authenticated deletion service.
