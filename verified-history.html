History of Masinloc, Zambales.

A source-led archive. Nothing is published here until a record, reference or properly attributed source stands behind it, and oral accounts stay marked as what they are.

Purpose

This is where we will gather the history that can be supported by records, credible references, archival material, or properly attributed evidence. Verified history will be separated clearly from oral accounts, local memory, interpretation, and claims that are still being checked.

Where the research has reached

Source-led articles on Masinloc history are published in the Masinloc Bulletin as the evidence for each one is established. The strongest so far:

Was Masinloc founded in 1572? · 1607 and the first mission church · 1649: when six caracoas came

Every article names the study, record or archive it rests on in our Sources & References directory.
