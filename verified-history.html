Verified History

History, with its evidence visible.

Masinloc's past comes to us through records, chronicles, local memory, scholarship and inherited stories. They are valuable for different reasons. We do not pretend they all carry the same kind of proof.

Our method

A useful local archive should be confident about what it knows and equally clear about what it does not.

Documented

Used when the event or claim is supported by documentary research, public records, established historical sources or source material strong enough to state the event directly.

Local chronicle

Used for community histories and teaching records that preserve important local memory but have not yet been independently corroborated at the same documentary level. These accounts remain visible because uncertainty is not a reason to erase memory.

Historical context

Used when a national, regional or international event shaped the conditions around Masinloc but should not be presented as though it happened only within the municipality.

This archive can change.

If a stronger record corrects a date, name or interpretation, the correction should improve the timeline rather than threaten it. History is not made more credible by pretending the first version was perfect.
